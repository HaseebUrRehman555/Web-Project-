Pro Equipment Guide

Compare the two main racket types and find the best fit for your game.

Head-Heavy Racket

Built for power. The weight sits towards the top of the frame, so every smash and clear hits harder.

  • Material: High-Modulus Carbon Graphite
  • Best for: attacking and back-court players
  • Strength: powerful smashes and deep clears
  • Trade-off: slower in fast exchanges

Balanced Racket

Built for all-round play. The weight is spread evenly, so the racket feels quick and easy to control.

  • Material: High-Modulus Carbon Graphite
  • Best for: doubles and all-round players
  • Strength: fast defence and quick drives
  • Trade-off: slightly less smash power

String Tension

30 lbs

Competitive players choose 30 lbs string tension for crisp control. The tighter strings give a clean, sharp feel on every shot, so placement at the net and in drop shots becomes more accurate.

It is a great balance of control and power, and it is why many club players string their rackets at this level.
