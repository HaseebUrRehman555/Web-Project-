Lahore Tournaments

Click a photo to read the news report, or join a tournament with one click.

National Badminton Championship

Venue: Iqbal Park Sports Complex, Lahore

The 50th edition brought over 200 players from 12 units of the Pakistan Badminton Federation to Lahore.

All-Pakistan Ranking Tournament

Venue: PHA Sports Complex, Samanabad, Lahore

More than 150 players competed in singles, doubles, mixed, veterans and team categories.

Aamer Hayat Khan Rokhri Invitational

Venue: New Khan Sports Board, Lahore

An All-Pakistan invitational where provinces and departments like Wapda and Railways send their teams.

Punjab Inter-District Championship

Venue: New Khan Court, Lahore

A knock-out contest where 25 districts of Punjab send male and female players to compete.

Inter-Department Badminton Tournament

Venue: Lahore Leads University

A university event run by the Sports Department, ending with trophies, medals and certificates for the winners.

SNGPL All-Pakistan Ranking Tournament

Venue: POF Wah Complex, Wah Cantt

The first edition of this ranking event, where Wapda and Army players won top honours in all categories.
